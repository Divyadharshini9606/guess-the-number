About GuessQuest

GuessQuest is a free online number guessing game built using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. Your goal is to discover the secret number using helpful clues before you run out of attempts.

How to play

Choose your difficulty level and enter a number within the displayed range. If your guess is too high or too low, the game gives you a hint. Use the clues carefully to find the correct answer and earn points.

Game features

  • Three difficulty levels: Easy, Medium, and Hard.
  • Helpful hints and guess history.
  • Score tracking and saved personal best.
  • Responsive design for desktop and mobile devices.
